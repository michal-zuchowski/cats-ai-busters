# Level 01 — The Blind Spot (planned)

**Place:** the data center from the intro, minutes after the CATCOM call.
**Goal:** interrupt the AI's first infrastructure command before it leaves
the building, without revealing the feline agency. Humans remain oblivious;
they are background characters, not enemies.

The black screen after the call fades into the service corridor. A short
message on the cat's CATCOM collar reads: `STOP THE RELAY. STAY UNSEEN.`
The gray tabby agent is already inside the data center. Three fixed screens
form one continuous route; crossing the right edge advances to the next
screen, and crossing the left edge returns to the previous one.

1. **Service corridor — learn the cameras.** A single AI-controlled camera
   scans a predictable arc across the floor. The first rack casts a safe
   blind spot. Walking in its shadow is safe; crossing the lit strip while
   the camera points elsewhere leads to the next doorway. A marking on the
   floor and the moving cone teach the rule without a text tutorial.
2. **Server aisle — create an opening.** Two camera sweeps overlap near the
   exit. A maintenance switch in the safe zone briefly points one camera at
   an empty wall. Press the action button beside the switch, then cross the
   aisle before it resets. The other camera continues its regular sweep;
   rack shadows still provide shelter. The timer starts only when the
   switch is pressed and can be retried indefinitely.
3. **Relay cabinet — act, then escape.** The relay terminal is beside the
   AI's wall monitor from the intro. Approach from the safe side, press the
   action button and stay at the terminal for a short, visible progress
   sequence. A moving camera makes the timing matter, but a nearby rack
   provides a place to wait. The cat diverts the outgoing command to a
   closed test network: the screen changes from `CONTROL INFRASTRUCTURE`
   to `ROUTE: TEST / ISOLATED`. The player then reaches the service hatch
   at the right edge to finish the level.

**Failure and recovery:** If a camera sees the cat in its lit detection
area, the relay raises a local alert. The screen briefly flashes
`UNRECOGNIZED DEVICE`; the cat retreats to the entrance of the current
screen and that screen's switch/camera state resets. Completed earlier
screens remain accessible. There are no lives, combat, or timed game over.
Being seen does not tell the AI who rules the world; it only sees an
unclassified disturbance.

**Ending:** A human technician glances at the restored status lights,
shrugs, and carries on. CATCOM reports `PHASE 02 DELAYED. AGENCY UNDETECTED.`
The AI still believes it is competing only with humans. Cut to black; no
second level is specified here.

The first playable milestone is one complete run from the corridor to the
hatch, including camera detection, reset, switch, relay action and ending.
Dialogue, collectibles, combat and a larger mission map can wait.
